Style: beautifully crafted contemporary digital illustration, clean softly modeled 3D-like forms, delicate natural water detail, crisp sunlight, clear shapes and vivid but balanced colors. High-end art direction, gentle dimensionality, atmospheric but readable. NOT vintage, NOT engraved, NOT crosshatched, NOT sepia, no paper grain, no pen-and-ink outlines, no distressed texture, no oil-paint impasto. No neon/cyberpunk, no floating circuit graphics. Not a photograph. Maintain visual richness in the coastline without cluttering the large blue sea.

Absolutely NO text, no lettering, no logo, no UI, no frame, no watermark, no gradients used as a visible fade or text panel, no fog or white veil. Image must run naturally to all four edges.
